Three genuinely distinct directions — not three tints of one idea. All three carry the identical hero message and inherit the app's ADR 0013 tokens, so the comparison isolates direction, not palette. Judged in the browser, not as descriptions.

Recommendation

Ship B as the spatial and emotional frame; graft C's citation discipline in as the trust mechanism; use A's mono annotation strictly for measured values.

Each direction is strongest exactly where the others are weakest, and the strategy tells us which weakness is fatal. B alone proves nothing — fatal for persona 3. C alone moves nobody — fatal for personas 1 and 2. A alone is a dashboard — memorable to no one, and “brand distinctiveness” is a scored dimension.

The synthesis is not a compromise, because the three operate at different layers: B owns space and rhythm (the threshold at every section boundary, the inside/outside duotone), C owns the treatment of claims (nothing asserted without a checkable source), A owns the annotation of numbers (mono reserved exclusively for measured values, never for headings).

One device carries the site, per the Nothing principle: the threshold — the lit line between the noise and the work. It is the enemy (“the renegotiation”) and the promise (“the rest goes quiet”) rendered as a single, repeatable graphic idea that no competitor owns.

Written, built, not yet published.

There is no Mac download on this site today, and this page is not going to invent one. What exists is the work: macOS support is written and merged, and the release pipeline builds a signed .dmg and .zip for Intel and Apple silicon on every tagged release. The first one is v1.7.0. When it is published, this page becomes a download.

What "supported" had to mean first

Porting an Electron app to macOS is mostly free, which is exactly why bad Mac ports are common: the app runs, so it looks finished. The parts that are not free are the ones that touch the operating system directly, and each of these was a real defect rather than a missing nicety.

  • Fixed for the Mac build

    • ⌘C, ⌘V, ⌘X, ⌘Z, ⌘A and ⌘Q. macOS reads keyboard equivalents from the menu bar. LockinRa had removed its application menu — correct on Windows, and on a Mac it means you cannot copy or paste anywhere in the app, including its own text fields.
    • Launching your apps. A .app is a folder, not an executable. Workspaces now hand it to LaunchServices, which is also what gives the app its Dock entry and its environment.
    • Finding your apps. The workspace picker reads your Applications folders instead of returning an empty list.
    • Run at login starts LockinRa hidden, in the menu bar, rather than throwing a dashboard at you on every boot.
    • Now playing in the focus mini-player reads Music and Spotify, and never launches a player just to ask it a question.
    • Local AI finds Ollama wherever you installed it — the app bundle, Homebrew on either architecture, or a manual install.

  • Known limits

    • Window titles need Screen Recording permission. macOS treats the title of another app's window as screen content. Without the permission LockinRa still sees which app you are in, so app-level focus detection works and title-aware detection does not. It is asked for once, and refusing it degrades the feature rather than breaking the app.
    • The menu-bar icon is a colour tile, not a monochrome glyph. A template icon keeps only the silhouette, and the LockinRa mark is a filled square — it would render as a black block.
    • No auto-update on the first release. The .zip that makes updates possible is built and published from v1.7.0 onward, so updating into v1.7.0 is a manual download.

Signing and notarization

The build is configured for a Developer ID identity and Apple's notary service, with the hardened runtime on. That last part is not a formality: the hardened runtime blocks the just-in-time compilation that both the browser engine and the on-device speech runtime depend on, so the app declares exactly the exceptions it needs — JIT, the microphone, and permission to ask Music and Spotify what is playing. Nothing broader.

Whether the published .dmg is signed depends on the credentials present when it is built. An unsigned build is a development build and will not be offered here as a download — Gatekeeper would be right to stop you.

System requirements

  • macOS 11 Big Sur or newer, on Apple silicon or Intel.
  • 8 GB of RAM. 16 GB is more comfortable. No GPU required.
  • Roughly 254 MB installed. The local models are most of it.

Windows has a current build and is on v1.6.0. Linux has a working AppImage and is three versions behind until v1.7.0.

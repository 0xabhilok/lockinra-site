Support

There is no ticket system and no chat widget. Write to abhilokreddydev@gmail.com and you will usually hear back within a few business days.

Sending from your own mail client means the message goes straight to that inbox — there is no form handler in between, and nothing about your message touches this website.

Before you write

Three things solve most of what arrives:

  • “Browser not connected” while the extension is installed. Fixed in v1.5.1 — update to the current release. If it persists, quit every LockinRa window and start it once.
  • Desktop distraction detection isn't firing on Linux. It works on X11 but is unreliable on Wayland, which is the default on many distros. The browser-extension path still covers web distractions. See the Linux page.
  • A model won't download. Nothing is downloaded until you pick one, and the smaller models are CPU-only. If a pull stalls, restart the app and try a smaller model first.

What LockinRa is

A local-first focus guardian: capture tasks by voice or text, run focus sessions with real interventions, track habits, take notes, and see where the time actually went — all stored on your own device.

  • Works offline. No account required.
  • On-device voice transcription and on-device AI by default.
  • Integrations (Google, Microsoft, GitHub, Slack) and cloud AI are opt-in and off until you connect them.

Diagnostics

If a bug needs logs, the app can export a diagnostic bundle. You review and edit it before it leaves your machine — nothing is sent automatically, and there is no crash reporter.

Privacy

We don't track you, show ads, or sell your data. The full detail, including what is not encrypted, is on the privacy page.
